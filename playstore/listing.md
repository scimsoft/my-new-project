## App Name

## Short Description (80 chars max)
Share anything instantly — just touch phones together. Fast, easy, wireless.

## Full Description (4000 chars max)
TapShare makes sharing between phones as simple as a touch. Just select what you want to share, bring two phones together, and it transfers instantly.

🤝 TAP TO SHARE
No QR codes, no pairing, no Wi-Fi passwords. Simply touch phones together (using NFC) and your content transfers automatically via a high-speed WiFi Direct connection.

📱 SHARE FROM ANY APP
TapShare appears in Android's share menu. Share a photo from your Gallery, a link from Chrome, a document from Files — anything, from any app.

⚡ FAST TRANSFERS
Small items like text and links transfer instantly via NFC. Larger files like photos, videos, and documents use WiFi Direct for speeds up to 250 Mbps — no internet connection required.

📦 SHARE ANYTHING
• Text & messages
• URLs & web links
• Photos & images
• Videos
• Documents & files
• Contacts

🔒 PRIVATE & SECURE
Transfers happen directly between devices over WiFi Direct — your data never touches any server. No account needed, no data collection.

🔔 SMART NOTIFICATIONS
Real-time transfer progress with notifications. Know exactly when your file has been sent or received.

📲 AUTO-LAUNCH
The receiver's phone automatically opens TapShare when it detects an incoming share — no need to manually open the app first.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Select what you want to share (or share from any app)
2. Touch phones together (back-to-back)
3. Done! Content transfers automatically

REQUIREMENTS:
• Both phones need NFC enabled
• TapShare installed on both devices
• Android 7.0 or later
